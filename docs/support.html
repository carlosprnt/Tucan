Free trial & subscription

Tucan Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. After that it’s Annual €9.99/year or Monthly €1.99/month. You can manage or cancel anytime in App Store → your account → Subscriptions.

Restore a purchase

If you’ve already subscribed, open the paywall and tap Restore purchases.

Delete your account

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your habits and history.
